# Entity Matching - Manual Investigation

**Investigation date:** 22 November 2026

## 1. VSCENE LIMITED

**Company number:** 10203912  
**VAT number:** GB243555507  
**Discovery source:** Company Check  
**HMRC verification:** Valid  
**Entity matching status:** Verified match

### Findings

The current Companies House registered office is 112 Smithurst Road, Giltbrook, Nottingham, NG16 2UP.

However, the company's incorporation document (IN01, May 2016) records its original address as 32 Eldon Road, Beeston, Nottingham, NG9 6DZ.

HMRC returns Unit 32, Eldon Road, Beeston, Nottingham, NG9 6DZ.

The historical address matches the HMRC address apart from a minor formatting difference. An AD01 filing from March 2026 confirms the subsequent registered office change.

**Conclusion:** The VAT is valid, the legal name matches exactly, and the historical address provides strong evidence of a correct entity match.

## 2. MODULAR EXPRESS GROUP LTD

**Company number:** 11164986  
**VAT number:** GB306701433  
**Discovery source:** Company Check  
**HMRC verification:** Valid  
**Entity matching status:** Manual review required

### Findings

HMRC returns an address in Newry, Northern Ireland, while Companies House records an address in London.

The company's filing history shows:

- **January 2018 - IN01:** Incorporated as TEMPO HOUSING MODULAR UK LTD, with a registered office at Chiswick Park, London.
- **January 2024 - RP05:** Registered office changed by Companies House to its default address in Cardiff.
- **August 2024 - AD01:** Registered office changed back to Chiswick Park, London.

None of the inspected filings establishes a connection with the Newry address returned by HMRC.

**Conclusion:** The VAT is valid and the legal name matches exactly, but the address discrepancy remains unresolved.

## 3. Key Findings

- VAT validity and legal-entity matching are separate checks.
- Historical Companies House filings can help resolve address discrepancies.
- A valid VAT with an unresolved identity discrepancy should be flagged for manual review.
- Missing VAT information does not prove that a company is not VAT-registered.
